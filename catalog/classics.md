# Foundational books

Works that created or redirected cognitive psychology. Read these for *shape of the field*, not as the latest empirical review.

## The cognitive revolution and its immediate aftermath

### Neisser, U. (1967). *Cognitive Psychology*
Often called the first true textbook of the field. Organizes cognition as information flowing inward from sensation through reconstruction to thought and memory. Historically indispensable; empirically dated.

### Miller, G. A., Galanter, E., & Pribram, K. H. (1960). *Plans and the Structure of Behavior*
TOTE units and the idea that behavior is organized by hierarchical plans — a bridge out of strict behaviorism.

### Neisser, U. (1976). *Cognition and Reality*
Neisser's later ecological turn: laboratory tasks can miss how perception and memory work in the world.

### Gardner, H. (1985). *The Mind's New Science: A History of the Cognitive Revolution*
Still the best single narrative of how philosophy, linguistics, computer science, anthropology, and psychology fused.

### Newell, A., & Simon, H. A. (1972). *Human Problem Solving*
The information-processing manifesto in book length. Protocol analysis, GPS, search in problem spaces.

## Vision, computation, architecture

### Marr, D. (1982). *Vision*
Three levels of analysis (computational, algorithmic, implementational). Still the model for how to theorize a cognitive system.

### Broadbent, D. E. (1958). *Perception and Communication*
Filter theory of attention; early information-theory psychology.

### Baddeley, A. D. (multiple editions). *Working Memory* / *Working Memory, Thought, and Action*
From the 1974 phonological-loop / visuospatial-sketchpad / central-executive model to later refinements (episodic buffer).

## Language, development, culture

### Chomsky, N. (1957/1965 and later). *Syntactic Structures*; *Aspects of the Theory of Syntax*
Not "cognitive psychology textbooks," but the linguistic shock that made poverty-of-the-stimulus and mental grammar central.

### Pinker, S. (1994). *The Language Instinct*
Accessible synthesis of nativist psycholinguistics for a wide audience.

### Piaget, J. — *The Origins of Intelligence in Children* and related works
Developmental constructivism; still required context even where the stage model has been revised.

### Vygotsky, L. S. (1978). *Mind in Society*
Internalization, zone of proximal development, culture as constitutive of higher cognition.

### Tomasello, M. — *The Cultural Origins of Human Cognition*; *A Natural History of Human Thinking*
Shared intentionality as the specifically human cognitive adaptation.

## Memory and the self

### James, W. (1890). *The Principles of Psychology*
Primary source for attention, the stream of thought, habit, and the empirical self. Public-domain in many jurisdictions.

### Bartlett, F. C. (1932). *Remembering*
Schema theory and reconstructive memory; the War of the Ghosts.

### Tulving, E. — collected essays / *Elements of Episodic Memory*
Episodic vs. semantic memory; encoding specificity; autonoetic consciousness.

### Schacter, D. L. (1996). *Searching for Memory*
Lab and clinic, written for a serious general reader.

## Judgment

### Kahneman, D. (2011). *Thinking, Fast and Slow*
Public synthesis of dual-process work and heuristics-and-biases. Read alongside the original Tversky & Kahneman papers, which are tighter.

### Thaler, R. H., & Sunstein, C. R. (2008). *Nudge*
Applied choice architecture; more behavioral economics than core cog psych, but downstream of the same literature.
