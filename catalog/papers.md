# Landmark papers (bibliographic index)

Prefer the publisher PDF via your library, DOI, or legal open repositories (PubMed Central, PsyArXiv, author's site when the author has posted an allowed version).

## Information processing and capacity

- Miller, G. A. (1956). The magical number seven, plus or minus two. *Psychological Review, 63*, 81–97.
- Broadbent, D. E. (1958). *Perception and Communication* (book; see classics).
- Sperling, G. (1960). The information available in brief visual presentations. *Psychological Monographs*.
- Sternberg, S. (1966). High-speed scanning in human memory. *Science*.
- Atkinson, R. C., & Shiffrin, R. M. (1968). Human memory: A proposed system and its control processes.

## Attention

- Cherry, E. C. (1953). Some experiments on the recognition of speech, with one and with two ears. *JASA*. (Cocktail party)
- Treisman, A. M. (1960). Contextual cues in selective listening.
- Posner, M. I. (1980). Orienting of attention. *QJEP*.
- Treisman, A. M., & Gelade, G. (1980). A feature-integration theory of attention. *Cognitive Psychology*.
- Stroop, J. R. (1935). Studies of interference in serial verbal reactions.

## Memory

- Baddeley, A. D., & Hitch, G. (1974). Working memory.
- Craik, F. I. M., & Lockhart, R. S. (1972). Levels of processing.
- Tulving, E., & Thomson, D. M. (1973). Encoding specificity.
- Godden, D. R., & Baddeley, A. D. (1975). Context-dependent memory in two natural environments (divers).
- Loftus, E. F., & Palmer, J. C. (1974). Reconstruction of automobile destruction.
- Roediger, H. L., & McDermott, K. B. (1995). Creating false memories (DRM paradigm).

## Language and thinking

- Chomsky, N. (1959). Review of Skinner's *Verbal Behavior*. *Language*.
- Swinney, D. A. (1979). Lexical access during sentence comprehension (cross-modal priming).
- Tversky, A., & Kahneman, D. (1974). Judgment under uncertainty: Heuristics and biases. *Science*.
- Tversky, A., & Kahneman, D. (1981). The framing of decisions and the psychology of choice. *Science*.
- Kahneman, D., & Tversky, A. (1979). Prospect theory. *Econometrica*.

## Vision and representation

- Gibson, J. J. (key papers and *The Ecological Approach to Visual Perception*, 1979).
- Marr, D., & Nishihara, H. K. (1978). Representation and recognition of the spatial organization of three-dimensional shapes.
- Goodale, M. A., & Milner, A. D. (1992). Separate visual pathways for perception and action.

## Architecture and computation

- Turing, A. M. (1950). Computing machinery and intelligence. *Mind*.
- Newell, A., & Simon, H. A. (1976). Computer science as empirical inquiry: Symbols and search. *CACM*.
- Rumelhart, D. E., McClelland, J. L., and the PDP group (1986). *Parallel Distributed Processing* (volumes).

Create `notes/author-year-short-title.md` from `notes/_template-paper.md` and add one line here with a relative link.
