# Graduate / deep path

Use this after a survey course or the beginner path.

## Core primary books

1. Neisser (1967) + Neisser (1976) — watch the ecological turn happen in one author.
2. Marr (1982) — levels of analysis until they are automatic.
3. Newell & Simon (1972) — problem spaces; then sample ACT-R or a modern cognitive architecture paper.
4. Baddeley monograph + Cowan / Engle papers — working memory as a live argument, not a diagram.
5. A current handbook chapter set (attention, memory, language, thinking) from *The Oxford Handbook of Cognitive Psychology* or similar.

## Methods you should be able to read

- Signal detection theory at a conceptual level (hits, FAs, d', criterion)
- Reaction-time logic (additive factors, race vs. serial debates — enough to be cautious)
- Eye-tracking and ERP component logic
- Pre-registration and replication culture after ~2011

## Paper circuit (repeat per topic)

1. The citation classic.
2. A major challenge or failed replication / boundary paper.
3. A computational or neural model paper.
4. A 5-year review.
